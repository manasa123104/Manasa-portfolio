Cover Letter

Dear Hiring Team,

How do you tell whether an object tracker is doing its job when no one has labeled the video? My graduate capstone at George Mason University started with that question. On the IMAGE DETECTIVES team, I helped build a real-time evaluation system using YOLOv8 and Norfair. We developed measures for how consistently the system detected objects and kept their identities over time, then built a dashboard to make those measures visible as the video ran.

That project brought together the work I enjoy most: writing software, deciding what to measure, and making the results usable. In my part-time AI Developer internship at Strong Point, I helped build multi-agent workflows and connect them to GitHub. At Giggso Inc., I worked part-time as an AI/ML Engineer Intern, supporting sales intelligence workflows and integrating APIs with machine learning. My earlier Java and SQL work gave me a foundation in building and testing the services that support these systems.

I hold an M.S. in Data Analytics Engineering from George Mason University. I’m interested in data analytics, machine learning, and software engineering roles where I can help a team build a system and figure out how well it works in practice. I’d welcome a conversation about what your team is building and how I could contribute.

Sincerely,
Manasa Rajagopal Madabushi
